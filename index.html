About

I'm an Electronics & Communication Engineering graduate (VTU Belagavi, CGPA 8.0) who builds things at the boundary of hardware and software — the layer where a register write becomes a voltage on a pin.

My six months at DRDO NSTL were spent writing bare-metal firmware for ARM Cortex-M3 and AVR ATxmega targets: interrupt-driven UART/SPI/I2C drivers and navigation-communication protocol stacks, work that earned me a Letter of Recommendation from a Scientist-'B' there. Outside of that, I've designed and verified a custom AXI4-Lite peripheral in Verilog, and built a mixed-signal DAC system accurate to 0.037% of full scale.

I'm currently a Trainee AV Design Engineer at Online Instruments (India) Ltd, working AV system design end-to-end — but the pull toward embedded systems, firmware, and silicon-adjacent work is what got this site built in the first place.

PrecisionDAC — Mixed-Signal Output System

Embedded CAVR ATxmega128A1U TI DAC81408SPIIEEE 754
Problem
Deliver a high-accuracy analog output from a floating-point setpoint over a resource-constrained AVR MCU.
Approach
Wrote Embedded C firmware to convert IEEE 754 floating-point values into the DAC81408's register format and drive it over SPI, with a serial link for setpoint updates.
Challenges
Getting bit-exact floating-point-to-register conversion right, and debugging the analog output path against expected values on a bench scope.
Result
0.037% full-scale error, verified against reference measurements.
